# Aircraft inspection: scoped UI evidence review

Reviewed 2026-10-04. This is a scoped design review, not an exhaustive literature survey or validation of our UI. Earlier maintenance literature, FD001 splits, qualification limits and evaluation protocols remain authoritative. The supplied aircraft references are inspiration; no actual reference screenshots were available in this session, so the implementation follows the written direction rather than claiming a pixel comparison.

| Source and actual inspection depth | Supported principle | Application and limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Karagappa et al., 2024, Enhancing Uncertainty Communication in Time Series Predictions](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.12365v1): full HTML methods/results/conclusions inspected | Interpretation depends on representation, viewers and information needs | Put interval units/meaning beside the estimate. The forecasting/public-health study does not validate our engineering dot-and-whisker UI; that representation is a design choice matching available bounds, not a density model. |
| [Moenck et al., 2024, Digital twins in aircraft production and MRO](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13272-024-00740-y): publisher abstract/introduction, concepts and application boundaries inspected | Useful applications connect scoped product/process information; holistic aircraft twins face integration barriers | Connect inspection evidence to planning/resources. Our illustrative asset and simulated histories do not satisfy physical-twin validation. |
| [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): relevant keyboard, focus, contrast, motion and target-size criteria consulted | Operable controls, readable states and alternatives | Named 44px controls, focus, table alternatives, reduced motion and non-WebGL selection. These choices and automated tests are not a conformance certification. |
| [NN/g, complex application design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/complex-application-design/): article guidance consulted | Complex work needs intelligible context and workflow | Progressive record details and exact-proposal review. Foundational guidance does not establish measured task-time improvement here. |
| [R3F scaling performance](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/scaling-performance): official on-demand/reuse guidance; [v9 migration](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/tutorials/v9-migration-guide) | Bound rendering work and align React compatibility | R3F 9 with React 19, demand frames and shared GLB resources. Device/browser measurements still required. |

The design thesis is that spatial component selection, quantitative 2D evidence and resource-aware planning form an understandable decision path. Success must be assessed with users of this prototype. The current acceptance protocol asks users to identify the selected aircraft/component, explain the estimate and interval, find quality/mandatory constraints, review a proposal, change a supply assumption and locate approval/reservation history. No participant study, task-success percentage or usability improvement is claimed.
