# Release-v2 scientific evidence

The selected serving model remains `customer-trial-v1`, a fitted gradient-boosting RUL regressor with stored transformations and split-conformal calibration. No LLM or neural-model promotion was introduced. The model reads supported FD001 operating settings and generic sensor histories through the selected cutoff; the separate policy decides review urgency. It cannot identify a mechanical fault or clear an aircraft.

## Selected-model endpoint reproduction

`artifacts/release-v2/official-endpoints.json` evaluates all 100 official truncated test endpoints. Capped-target MAE **8.72198 cycles**, RMSE **11.70987**, summed asymmetric score **207.2660**; uncapped secondary MAE **9.76934**, RMSE **13.18152**, asymmetric score **245.3623**. Nominal 90% intervals covered **98/100**, with mean width **58.23073 cycles** and one-sided 95% binomial coverage lower bound **0.93838**. Stage groups include small samples and are not conditional-coverage guarantees. These reproduce the previously inspected endpoint benchmark; no endpoint was used to tune this run. Endpoint labels do not supply missing future sensor trajectories.

## New complete-life experiment

`configs/lifecycle_release.yaml` was frozen before a new fit: 50 fit / 15 selection-validation / 15 calibration / 20 lifecycle engines, engine-disjoint, seed 26250, 30-cycle minimum/window, target cap 125. The split is written before fitting; scaling/support envelopes use fit units only. Calibration and snapshot evaluation sample one seeded uniform cutoff per engine. Prefix replay strides five cycles and includes the final cycle; end-of-life is evaluator truth only. The 20 engines were used in older experiments, so this is a holdout **from the new model**, not historically untouched or an independent external study.

The two runs in `artifacts/models/lifecycle-release-v2-provenance` and `lifecycle-release-v2-reproduced` produced identical reports/artifact hashes (`artifacts/release-v2/lifecycle-provenance.json` and `lifecycle-reproduced.json`). MAE **10.59333**, RMSE **14.11084**, asymmetric score **77.05036**, versus the fit-only constant baseline MAE **31.44490**, RMSE **36.65048**, score **1241.87620**. Coverage was **20/20** and mean width **86.51194 cycles**. Engine bootstrap MAE interval was **6.68128–14.78299**. The all-success coverage bootstrap degenerates at 1.0; it does not establish perfect or conditional coverage. Small sample size, exchangeability/cutoff distribution, simulation and distribution shift limit interpretation. Width exceeds the older 62.5-cycle demonstrator utility bound, so this candidate was **not promoted**.

The demo-v2 policy uses warning ≤45 cycles, critical ≤20, clear margin 8, two fresh warning samples, freshness/cooldown 10 cycles; critical escalation is immediate. Corrected same-cutoff evidence cannot add persistence or clear an episode but can escalate critical concern. Withheld evidence retains open concerns; acknowledgement is separate from resolution. Existing legacy concerns retain identity when first processed by the new policy.

Over 3,489 observed eligible engine cycles, the 45-cycle policy detected 20/20 benchmark events, mean first actionable lead **32.7 cycles**, **2 early false episodes** (**0.57323/1,000 observed cycles**) and **43 state/recommendation transitions**. Those transitions are not measured schedule revisions. Sensitivity at warning thresholds 30/45/60 yielded false episodes 0/2/10 and mean leads 22.95/32.7/39.95; all detected 20 events. This diagnostic did not select or retune the policy. Censored trajectories with unknown future failure are not counted as verified false alarms. Operational missed/false-alert and cost limits remain unaccepted.

## Explanations and input robustness

`explanations.json`: identical-input repeat differences and independent single-feature intervention errors were zero. Mean top-ten overlap **0.9442**, mean cosine **0.9957**, mean absolute sensitivity change **0.01655 cycles**, mean prediction change **0.4076 cycles** under the recorded validation probes. This tests deterministic implementation/interventions, not additive or physical causation. Correlated engineered-feature replacement can leave the data manifold; application stability limits are not frozen.

`robustness.json` retains the original validation-only intervention/imputation research protocol. Clean MAE **9.42227**; 3% missingness +**1.06858** cycles under that offline imputation experiment; eight-cycle outage withheld all 15 cases. Noise and fit-range-clipped setting shift remained diagnostic. These are **not serving acceptance**: the current serving path conservatively withholds any missing history, short/nonfinite/fully-flat sensor history and declared unsupported operating settings. New models record fit-only setting envelopes; the immutable legacy model uses its narrower recorded support plus setting_3=100. This is not a validated general out-of-distribution detector. Training/serving parity, future-cutoff exclusion and corrupt weights/transforms/calibrator handling are tested separately.

## Sources and review limits

[NASA C-MAPSS](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/cmapss-jet-engine-simulated-data) is the simulated source; acquisition hashes/attribution are in `data/sources.yaml`. The portal did not establish a commercial dataset license in the retained acquisition review. [Javanmardi and Hüllermeier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.14612) motivates conformal RUL evaluation; review here covered the abstract and author implementation references, not a full reproduction of that paper. [de Pater and Mitici](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S095183202100288X) concerns imperfect RUL and limited spares; the supplied prompt's numbered R1–R15 bibliography was absent, so its exact R4/R15 papers were not treated as confirmed full-text reviews. Project thresholds and benefits require project evidence and customer agreement.
