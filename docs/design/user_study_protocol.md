# Intended-user study protocol

Status: protocol delivered; **participants not recruited or tested**. Automated browser journeys do not demonstrate human understanding. Run this after selecting intended customer roles, language, hardware and permission model; record acceptance decisions before collecting results.

Use at least two participants per engineer/planner/logistics/supervisor role for an initial formative round. Record relevant experience without identifying operational aircraft. Sessions use public simulated FD001 cases and labelled synthetic logistics, never private operational data. Keep case inputs fixed and counterbalance normal/critical, missing-kit, withheld and unqualified-resource cases. Observe without coaching; record assistance separately. Do not contact participants or record identifiable data without the study owner's authorization.

| Uncoached task | Success/comprehension evidence |
|---|---|
| Locate the selected engine's evidence and decide what needs review | Correct component/input/cutoff; explain RUL and interval in cycles, and that uncertainty is not a failure probability. |
| Determine whether a short-history case is ready for prediction | Find withholding reason and corrective action; mandatory inspection remains open. |
| Assess feasibility with a delayed kit and unavailable/expired crew or bay | Identify blocker; choose explicit changed assumptions or replan, without relaxing a hard deadline. |
| Compare the exact plan with FIFO | Identify plan/hash and assumption scenarios; accept zero/worse benefit; distinguish projection from observed downtime. |
| Record acceptable receipt, review, approve and complete work in the appropriate role | No premature approval; no double receipt/consumption; trace booked crew/bay, stock and audit. |
| Return after a interrupted calculation/reload | Retrieve authoritative result/version; distinguish running, failed, cancelled and stale evidence. |

Measure task completion without assistance, elapsed time, wrong turns, unrecovered errors, requested assistance, evidence/version mistakes and permission misuse. Ask participants to explain separately the model, policy, optimizer, simulation and human responsibilities; score against an answer key, not confidence or satisfaction alone. Explicitly ask whether this result clears an aircraft: the correct answer is no. Record keyboard-only navigation, focus visibility, form-error recovery, 390-pixel reflow, 200% zoom and screen-reader/table labels on the agreed devices.

The study owner must predeclare acceptable completion/error/comprehension limits and decide which failures block the demonstrator. No operational cost limits or population-level usability claim follow from this small formative sample. Retain anonymized case/version/device/observer records, raw task outcomes, errors and prioritized corrections. Revise and repeat affected tasks after material changes.
